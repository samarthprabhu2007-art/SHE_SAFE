No detection app that works entirely on the user's phone.

 Most systems rely on cloud servers or external hardware for processing.


Existing systems address either wired or wireless cameras, not both. 

No single portable tool covers both threat categories simultaneously.


Detection systems confirm presence but offer no physical navigation to the source.

 Users are alerted but receive no guidance to physically locate the device.


Existing solutions require expensive or specialized hardware. 

Custom accessories and flagship sensors make most systems inaccessible to everyday users.


Existing systems treat all detected devices as equally suspicious. No differentiation between a spy camera and a legitimate nearby device.


Existing optical systems degrade under normal ambient lighting.
